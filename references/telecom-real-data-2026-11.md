# Telecom / Defense: real QKD telemetry, real 5G and O-RAN attack traffic, teachers (read 2026-11-25)

On the owner's choice of 2026-11-25 (prefer practical large-scale REAL data to synthetic, include calibration and
preprocessing in the verification, and assess anything worth bringing to the encrypted paths), a survey read the
primary sources for the telecom page, and the agent then re-read each Zenodo record and arXiv entry relied on below
at its own API on 2026-11-25. Nothing was downloaded. Where a figure was read by the survey and not re-read, it says
so. Everything here is defensive: detectors and the data to test them, no attack tooling.

## What the page uses today

- **The QKD intrusion detector, synthetic:** telemetry generated by `qsh.qml_ids.attacks` with the eight classes of
  Al-Kuwari et al. (arXiv:2509.14282; seven attacks and normal). The published random forest, its CKKS student (a
  multinomial logistic regression distilled from the forest) and the QCNNs all train and score on it.
- **The network-layer detector, real:** 5G-NIDD (CC BY 4.0), gradient-boosted trees over 46 flow features after the
  leak and duplicate removal `references/5g-nidd-metric-profile.md` records. It has **no encrypted path**; the
  repository's plaintext linear baseline on it, over 36 features and 9 classes from 300,000 training rows, reaches
  macro-F1 0.969 (`models/metrics.json`).
- CIC-IDS2017 is declared and not implemented (its provider's pages conflict on terms, `DATA_LICENSES.md`).

## Real QKD data: none with attacks

Al-Kuwari et al.'s data is itself simulated (the paper's own words: a simulation-generated decoy-state BB84 dataset,
10,329 rows) and available only on request, with no data licence (survey). No public experimental QKD telemetry with
attack labels was found for 2024-2026; real attack captures in the literature stay unpublished. What exists is real
**honest** telemetry:

| Record | Read at | Licence | Size | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *QKD Network Characterisation and Coexistence Datasets* (TCD/CONNECT, IrelandQCI) | Zenodo 21792844 (2026-08-04) | CC BY 4.0 | 39 files, 2.2 MB | QBER and key rate against attenuation on five commercial systems, coexistence with injected classical light, and labelled perturbation runs (survey) |
| *Deploying QKD on the fiber links of the National Quantum Communication Infrastructure in Sweden* (KTH) | Zenodo 24080253 (2026-11-09), arXiv:2611.12279 | CC BY 4.0 | 896.7 MB | deployed-fibre characterisation and a one-month QKD session |
| *Performance Assessment of Fiber-Optic Railway Infrastructure QKD Testbed* (CTU Prague) | Zenodo 15873852 (2025-07-13) | CC BY 4.0 | 0.9 MB | an inter-city field link along railway tracks |

Such data can support a claim about **false alarms on real devices**, never about detecting real attacks.

The first two share authors with 5G-NIDD, so they test across testbeds, not across independent labs; NCSRD's is a
different lab. CSE-CIC-IDS2018's own page grants redistribution with a citation (`DATA_LICENSES.md`, read
2026-10-30); its corrected DistriNet release states no licence, so it could be used locally only. UNSW-NB15, TON_IoT
and the UQ NetFlow sets ask for the authors' agreement before commercial use (survey) and are not candidates.

## Teachers, calibration and practice

- Traffic foundation models (ET-BERT, YaTC, NetMamba, netFound) read raw packets; several have no code licence, and
  2025-2026 audits tie their scores to shortcuts such as address overlap (survey). A pre-registered 2026 study found a
  distilled student no better calibrated than a temperature-scaled student trained directly (arXiv:2609.31141,
  2026-09-25). So no foundation-model teacher is proposed; the project's own trees stay the teachers, with a directly
  trained, temperature-scaled control beside every distilled student.
- Practice: identifiers masked, duplicates removed, splits by time or session, whole attack families held out at a
  fixed false-positive rate, probability calibration (temperature or isotonic) and per-family reporting. For QKD
  monitors, the reference must be taken under matching operating conditions (Q-TRACE, arXiv:2611.28945, 2026-11-21).
- A deployment caution for every CKKS path: a reaction attack recovers a CKKS secret key from a client's one-bit
  reactions to crafted ciphertexts (arXiv:2610.21127, 2026-10-15, IEEE S&P 2027). The evaluator must never learn
  whether a decryption succeeded, nor any decrypted value it was not meant to receive.

## Options for the owner

1. **An encrypted path for the 5G-NIDD detector, and a cross-testbed check** (fetch about 450 MB: the NetsLab flow
   CSV and the VTT set; NCSRD's 620 MB optional). A linear student over the 46 standardised features
   ($`9 \times 46 + 9 = 423`$ parameters) and a quadratic one, each distilled from the trees and each against a directly
   trained, temperature-scaled control; CKKS scores on a held-out sample in the tab with the argmax in a garbled
   circuit; encrypted against plaintext agreement, per-class F1, held-out-family detection at 1 % FPR, calibration
   error before and after the temperature; the shared classes scored on the other testbeds without retraining.
2. **Real honest QKD telemetry for false alarms** (fetch 3.1 MB: the TCD and CTU records; KTH's 897 MB optional).
   Per-system expectations of QBER and key rate fitted on baseline sweeps, a residual or CUSUM monitor set to one false
   alarm per 1,000 windows on held-out honest rows, measured on the coexistence noise and the perturbation runs; the
   tab's synthetic-trained detector run on real honest rows as an out-of-distribution check. Under CKKS the residuals
   are sums and products; the threshold goes in the garbled circuit.
3. **CSE-CIC-IDS2018 with day-based splits and held-out families** (6.89 GB of processed CSVs), for a larger real
   corpus.

Not recommended: UNSW-NB15, TON_IoT, the UQ NetFlow sets; MAWI (research-only terms); sets without a licence or with
simulated radio; traffic foundation models as teachers; anything attack-side.
